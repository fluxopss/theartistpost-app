# House Manager Guide

For Robbie. This explains the admin tools in The Artist Post app: approving artists, handling reports, and what members see.

Items marked **Ask Jonathan** are not decided in the app yet. Check them before you act on them.

---

## What your admin pass lets you do

You see a **Manage the house** group in Settings. It has two tools:

- **Artist requests**: approve or revoke artists.
- **Moderation**: review reports from members.

Things to know:

- You can't suspend yourself or another admin from the queue.
- You can't delete accounts from the app. Members delete their own accounts (see below).
- Keep your admin account to yourself.

If you don't see **Manage the house**, your account isn't set as an admin. Ask Jonathan.

---

## Approving artist requests

1. Open **Settings → Manage the house → Artist requests**.
2. The **Pending** tab lists artists who asked to publish to The Wall. Each row shows their name, handle, email, and the date they joined.
3. Tap **Approve**, then **Approve** again to confirm. The artist can now publish to The Wall. They also become an artist in the app.
4. To take that back, open the **Approved** tab, tap **Revoke**, and confirm. They can't publish until you approve them again. Revoking doesn't change their member role.
5. If a list has more than one page, tap **Show older** at the bottom.

**Ask Jonathan:** what you should look at before approving (their work, their bio, a call?). The app doesn't show an applicant's work to you yet.

## Handling a report (aim for 24 hours)

Members report posts, Wall notes, community posts, and people with the `⋯` menu. Reports land in **Settings → Manage the house → Moderation**.

Only **open** reports appear there. Each card shows what was reported, the reason, how many people reported it, a preview, and any note the reporter wrote.

1. Open the card and read the preview. Open the item itself if you need more context.
2. Pick one action. The buttons on the card depend on what was reported:
   - **Hide** (Wall works, Wall notes, and community posts only): the item disappears for everyone else right away. Its reports close. Use it for content that breaks the Community Guidelines.
   - **Dismiss** (every report): nothing new is removed and the reports close. If the house had hidden a community post or a Wall note automatically, Dismiss brings it back.
   - **Suspend** (every report): the person can't post, comment, or like anymore. They can still sign in, read, and delete their own account. Suspend also hides the reported item and closes its reports.
   - **Profile reports** have no Hide button. For a profile, choose **Suspend** or **Dismiss**.
3. Confirm the action. Once it's done, the card leaves the queue.

**Auto-hide:** when three different people report the same item, it is hidden automatically. A Wall work is archived instead. Auto-hide doesn't close the reports, so they stay in the queue until you decide.

**Note:** Dismiss doesn't bring back a Wall work that was archived. **Ask Jonathan** how to restore one.

**Who answers reports:** Robbie Alvarez (founder) reviews reports within 24 hours. That is decided (2026-10-15), and the message members see stays as it is.

## Blocking, hiding, and suspending

| Tool | Who uses it | What it does | Who it affects |
|---|---|---|---|
| Block | Any member | Stops two people from seeing each other's posts, notes, and profiles. Removes follows both ways. | Just those two people |
| Hide | You, from a report | Removes one item from public view | The item only |
| Suspend | You, from a report | The person can't post, comment, or like | The whole account, read-only |

Blocking is for members. You don't need to block anyone to moderate.

## What members see

- **Community Guidelines.** Members read them during the welcome steps. They are approved as written (2026-10-15). **Ask Jonathan:** whether they should also be linked from Settings. Right now they are not.
- **Report and Block** in the `⋯` menu on posts, notes, and profiles.
- **Settings → Blocked** shows who a member has blocked, with a way to unblock.
- **"Let's keep it kind — please rephrase."** appears when the word filter stops a post or note.
- **"Thanks — the house team reviews reports within 24 hours."** appears after a report is sent.
- **Activity** shows follows, likes, support, replies, mentions, and comments.

**Artist payment usernames are coming.** Artists will get an optional spot on their profile for their personal payment usernames (such as Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal.me). It links out to those services. Payments happen outside the app. The app does no payment processing for these, and TAP takes nothing from them. Donations made through the app all go to The Artist Post.

## Deleting accounts

- **Members delete their own accounts.** They go to Settings, choose Delete account, and type DELETE. This removes their profile, follows, community posts, notifications, blocks, and reports they filed. It also removes the comments and likes they left on the Wall. A one-way hash of their email is kept for the record.
- **If someone emails you asking to delete their account**, the Delete account page on the website says the house will delete it for them. The app has no delete button for admins today.

  **Ask Jonathan** how email deletion requests are carried out before you promise anyone a date.

- Don't delete an account to settle a dispute. Use **Suspend** instead.

## How artists get approved

1. An artist joins from the **Join** screen, using the artist door. They give their name, email, handle, medium, and what they want to do with the house.
2. The request goes to your **Pending** tab. The artist sees "Artist approval pending" and can't publish yet.
3. You approve them. They can then publish to The Wall.

Members can post to the Community board without being approved. Approval only controls publishing to The Wall.

---

## What not to do

- **Never charge artists** to join, post, or show their work. The house is free for artists, and the Guidelines say so.
- **Don't approve or feature anyone just to fill the screen.** The app shows real people and real work only.
- **Don't use Hide to punish someone.** Hide the item when it breaks the Guidelines. Suspend only after you have looked at the report.
- **Don't share a reporter's name with the person they reported.** **Ask Jonathan** if you're unsure what to say to either side.
- **Don't copy members' emails** into mailing lists or other tools without asking first.
